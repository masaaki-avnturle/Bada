\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{\jp{要旨}.}\;
\jp{本稿は、リニア新幹線のオペレーティングシステムとしての生成AIを、量子コンピューター

We present a small generative-AI kernel, written in the conceptual \texttt{Bada}
operator language and lowered to C, that acts as the operating system of a
magnetically levitated train. Each control tick the kernel (i)~commands an
electromagnetic (EMS) levitation field so the lift force equals the car weight,
(ii)~budgets the coil Ohmic power as a normal-conductor \emph{alternative to
superconductivity}, (iii)~models an evacuated-tube ``no air resistance''
running mode, (iv)~trims a residual ``gravity resistance'' through the global
partial-integral manifold element $1/(x\log x)^2$, and (v)~tracks the Jones
polynomial of a thermal-energy body via a Kauffman-bracket state sum weighted by
a gamma-function factor. A $\pi$-softmax policy selects the control action and
writes telemetry to an Akashic TupleSpace. We report the measured operating
point (flux $B=2.106\,$T, amp-turns $NI=16{,}761$, lift $=441{,}299\,$N matching
the weight of a $45\,$t car) and state precisely which components are physical
and which are conceptual.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Magnetically levitated ground transport replaces wheel--rail contact with a
servo-controlled magnetic force. We ask a deliberately playful question in the
project's idiom: \emph{what would an ``operating system'' for such a vehicle look
like if it were written as a generative controller in the Bada quantum
language?} The answer is a five-subsystem kernel whose physical core (levitation,
power, drag) is real and whose speculative shell (a manifold ``gravity
resistance'' term, a knot-theoretic thermal invariant) is the repository's
conceptual motif. We keep the two layers explicitly separated (see the note
above and \S\ref{sec:scope}).

Bada expresses operator-algebra programs with four constructs used throughout
this work:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\texttt{<-} & \text{left non-commutative act } \pi(\chi,x)=[\,i\pi,\,f(x)\,]\\
\texttt{-<} & \text{manifold integral } \displaystyle\iint \frac{1}{(x\log x)^2}\,dx_m\\

The kernel \texttt{bada/maglev\_os.bada} instantiates one class per subsystem and
runs a single control tick \verb|BadaOS <- shinkansen|.

\section{Electromagnetic levitation (real physics)}
An EMS pole develops an attractive force across the air gap $g$. With $N I$
amp-turns,
\begin{equation}
B=\frac{\mu_0\,N I}{g},\qquad
F=\frac{B^2 A}{2\mu_0}=\frac{\mu_0 (N I)^2 A}{2 g^2},
\label{eq:lev}
\end{equation}
where $A$ is the pole area and $\mu_0=1.2566\times10^{-6}\,$H/m. The controller
solves $F=m g_0$ for the commanded amp-turns,
\begin{equation}
N I = g\sqrt{\frac{2 m g_0}{\mu_0 A}}.
\label{eq:ni}
\end{equation}
This is the honest core of what the marketing layer calls the ``anti-gravity
field'': the field simply balances weight so the car floats.

\section{A superconductor alternative}
Rather than a superconducting coil ($R\to0$), the kernel reports the Ohmic
power a \emph{normal} winding of $T$ turns would dissipate,
\begin{equation}
P = I^2 R = \left(\frac{N I}{T}\right)^{\!2} R,
\end{equation}
so an operating point can be chosen that keeps $P$ within the on-board power
budget. For the reference car ($NI=16{,}761$, $T=400$, $R=0.02\,\Omega$) we find
$P\approx 35\,$W per module.

\section{Aerodynamics and the ``no air resistance'' mode}
Drag is the textbook
\begin{equation}
F_{\text{drag}}=\tfrac12\,\rho\,C_d\,A_f\,v^2 .
\end{equation}
The only physical route to zero drag is to remove the air: an evacuated tube
drops $\rho\to0$. We report the removed fraction
$r=1-\rho/\rho_{\text{air}}$; at $500\,$km/h in vacuum, $F_{\text{drag}}=0$ and
$r=100\%$ versus sea level ($42{,}541\,$N).

\section{The general-relativity manifold layer (conceptual)}
\label{sec:manifold}
In real general relativity a free body follows a geodesic and there is no
``gravity resistance'' for a field to cancel. As a control-gain construct we
integrate the global partial-integral manifold element
\begin{equation}
d\mu(x)=\frac{1}{(x\log x)^2},\qquad
M=\iint\frac{1}{(x\log x)^2}\,dx_m\;\approx\;\sum_{i=2}^{n+1}\frac{1}{(i\log i)^2},
\end{equation}
and define a dimensionless cancellation ratio $c\in[0,1)$; the residual vertical
acceleration reported to the OS is $a_{\text{res}}=g_0(1-c)$. For $64$ steps
$M\approx 0.692$. \emph{This term is a knob, not new physics.}

\section{Jones polynomial of the thermal-energy body}
The recirculating coolant / eddy-heat loop of a levitation module is represented
as a knot diagram $D$. The Kauffman bracket is the state sum
\begin{equation}
\langle D\rangle=\sum_{s\in\{A,B\}^{n}} A^{\,a(s)-b(s)}\,d^{\,|s|-1},
\qquad d=-A^2-A^{-2},
\end{equation}
over all smoothings $s$ of the $n$ crossings, with $|s|$ the loop count. We fold
in a gamma-function global-partial (beta) weight depending on body temperature
$T$,
\begin{equation}
\beta(a,b)=\frac{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a+b)},\qquad
J_{\text{th}}=\langle D\rangle\,\beta\!\left(1+\tfrac{T}{300},\,1+\tfrac{300}{T}\right).
\end{equation}
For the trefoil model at $A=1.2$, $T=320\,$K we obtain
$\langle D\rangle=-6.9737$ and $J_{\text{th}}=-1.1623$.

\section{The generative-AI OS kernel}
The kernel scores five actions
$\{$raise, lower field; evacuate tube; trim manifold; hold$\}$ from the physics
error signals, normalises them to a common scale, and forms a policy with the
$\pi$-softmax
\begin{equation}
p_i=\frac{\exp(\ell_i\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}\,\pi)}{\sum_j\exp(\ell_j\,\hbar_{\text{eff}}\,\pi)},
\qquad \hbar_{\text{eff}}=0.35,
\end{equation}
selecting $\arg\max_i p_i$. Applying the action updates the gap, ambient density,
or cancellation ratio, and the tick's telemetry is pushed to
$\Omega$::\texttt{DATABASE}.

\section{Scope and limitations}
\label{sec:scope}
Physical and correct: Eqs.~\eqref{eq:lev}--\eqref{eq:ni}, the power budget, and
the drag model. Conceptual / metaphorical: the manifold ``gravity resistance''
term (\S\ref{sec:manifold}) and the mapping of heat flow to a knot. The
$\pi$-softmax kernel is a simulated autopilot, not a validated safety system and
not a trained large model. No hardware claim is made.

\section{Conclusion}
We packaged a maglev ``operating system'' as a small generative controller in
the Bada language, combining a correct electromagnetic-levitation core with the
project's manifold and knot-theoretic motifs, and reported a self-consistent
operating point. The value of the exercise is expository: it shows how far a
physically honest core can be wrapped in the Bada operator idiom while keeping
the speculative layer clearly labelled.
