Title: Optimizing Neural Network Training
Abstract: Effective training of neural networks requires careful tuning of hyperparameters (learning rate, batch size), appropriate initialization, and use of regularization (dropout, weight decay).
Section: Optimization Methods
- SGD: simple stochastic gradient descent; may require momentum for stability.
- Adam: adaptive optimizer; commonly used with default betas (0.9, 0.999).
Recommendations: start with a moderate learning rate, monitor validation loss, use early stopping.
